# Mock: timed practical (2/2)

> **Goal:** Finish your mock piece in one hour: develop and refine the main areas, resolve the whole image and present it so it fully realises your intentions (AO4).

## Before you start

- **Material:** your unfinished work from the previous session, your plan and timing sheet, your preparatory studies (look only), all materials, a timer.
- Same conditions as before: quiet, unaided, no new research, no phone except as a clock.
- Read your "next session" note from last time.
- If your teacher runs the mock differently (more or fewer hours), ask what to do and adapt the plan below proportionally.

## Exam format reminder

- The real sustained focus period is 10 hours in up to four sessions; this is the **final stage** of your timed work.
- AO4 (18 marks): "Present a personal and meaningful response that realises intentions and demonstrates understanding of visual language."
- The assessment grid rewards a response that is personal, **realised** (finished), shows control of **formal elements** and a developing **personal style**, with technical competence.
- Work must not be altered after submission.

## How to do it

1. **0–5 min** – Look at your work, plan and note. Mark what still needs doing in order of importance (focal point, then supporting areas, then edges).
2. **5–25 min** – **Main development.** Strengthen the focal point and the largest supporting areas: modelling of form, colour mixing, texture and the tone pattern.
3. **25–40 min** – **Refine.** Add detail where the eye will look. Adjust contrast. Fix any problems (unbalanced area, weak edge).
4. **40–50 min** – **Finishing.** Final details and highlights; clean up edges; simplify busy parts rather than over-working.
5. **50–55 min** – **Final review.** Step back; compare with your design and your intention sentence. Are the mood and message clear? One last adjustment only.
6. **55–60 min** – **Present and photograph.** Mount or arrange as required, photograph the final work, and write a short reflection (4 lines: what worked, what you would change, which AO was your strongest, which weakest).

## Exam technique for this paper

- **Finish.** An unfinished outcome weakens AO4; simplify if needed.
- **Don't overwork:** stop when the image works.
- Check **visual language**: is there a clear focal point, intentional colour and tonal range, balanced composition?
- Link back to **intentions**: does it say what you planned?
- Keep the **personal style**: your own subject and choices, even when influenced by artists.
- Pay attention to **presentation**: clean edges, tidy mounting; for 3D, plan four photographs from different angles.
- If short of time, use your fallback: simplify the background but keep the focal point.

## Checklist while marking

Mark your own work against AO4 (and note how your studies support the others):
- [ ] **Personal and meaningful:** is it clearly my idea, not a copy?
- [ ] **Realises intentions:** does it match my plan and intention sentence?
- [ ] **Visual language:** line, tone, colour, shape, texture and composition used deliberately?
- [ ] **Technical control:** media handled competently; no rushed areas?
- [ ] **Personal style:** my own choices evident?
- [ ] **Resolved:** finished, with no blank areas?
- [ ] **Link to studies:** shows ideas from AO1 research and AO2 experiments.
- [ ] **Presented:** neat and ready.

Use the grid descriptors, from "limited" to "exceptional": decide whether your work just, mostly or fully meets each band, then give a mark from 1 to 18 for AO4.

## Quick-fire revision

- Finish and resolve: examiners mark what is on the sheet.
- AO4 looks for: personal, meaningful, realises intentions, visual language.
- Review against your intention sentence.
- Focal point strong; supporting areas simplified.
- Check contrast, balance and colour harmony.
- Don't add new ideas at the end.
- Presentation: tidy edges, mounting, photographs of 3D work (about four angles, mounted on A2).
- Work must not be altered after submission.
- Submit three A2 supporting sheets and one A2 final outcome sheet.
- Note what you would change: use it in your next sketchbook decisions.
- Mark bands: limited, basic, emerging competent, competent and consistent, confident and assured, exceptional.
- 18 marks per AO.
