# Past paper · Paper 2 (multiple choice)

> **Goal:** sit a full Paper 2 in 45 minutes under exam conditions, mark it, and log every wrong answer by topic.

## Before you start

- **Paper**: a Cambridge IGCSE Biology 0610 Paper 2 (Extended multiple choice, components 21/22/23) from a recent series you have **not** done — e.g. May/June 2025 or Oct/Nov 2025; if not available, May/June or Oct/Nov 2024. Papers from 2023 onwards match the current syllabus content most closely; older papers may contain questions on topics no longer tested.
- **Mark scheme** (the answer key) for the same component, kept out of sight.
- **Timer** set to 45 minutes.
- Pencil (answers on the paper or a printed answer grid), eraser, ruler. A calculator is allowed.
- Your error log (notebook or file) for the last part of the hour.

## Exam format reminder

- **45 minutes, 40 marks, 30 %** of the IGCSE.
- **40 multiple-choice questions**, four options **A–D**, one mark each, no negative marking.
- Questions follow the syllabus order roughly, from characteristics of living things to biotechnology and human influences.
- That is about **1 minute per question**, with 5 minutes spare to check.

## How to do it

1. **0–45 min**: sit the paper. No notes, no phone. Answer every question.
2. **45–55 min**: mark with the key. Write the total out of 40.
3. **55–60 min**: for each wrong answer, write one line in the error log (see the checklist). If time runs out, finish the log at the start of your next session.

## Exam technique for this paper

- **Read the stem fully before looking at the options.** Many wrong answers come from stopping at a familiar word.
- Watch for **NOT**, **least**, **most likely**, **correct** and **incorrect** — underline them.
- **Cover the options** and predict the answer first; then find it. If your answer is not there, re-read the question.
- **Eliminate** clearly wrong options, then decide between two. Cross them out on the paper.
- **Tables of ticks and crosses** (e.g. "which row is correct for…"): go column by column — decide each column independently and eliminate rows.
- **Diagrams** (heart, leaf, eye, nephron): label the diagram yourself in pencil before reading the options.
- **Calculations** (magnification, percentage change, energy efficiency, rates): do the working in the margin; check units (mm vs µm).
- **Graphs**: read both axes and units; check if a question asks for the value or the change.
- **Genetics**: draw a quick Punnett square — never do crosses in your head.
- If stuck after a minute, put your best guess, circle the number and come back. Never leave a blank.
- Use the last 5 minutes to re-check circled questions only; don't change answers without a clear reason.

## Checklist while marking

- Mark strictly against the key; there is no "nearly right" in multiple choice.
- For **each wrong answer** record in the error log:
  - question number and **syllabus topic** (e.g. "Q17 – Transport in plants"),
  - the answer you gave and the correct one,
  - **why** you got it wrong: (a) didn't know the fact, (b) misread the question, (c) calculation slip, (d) confused two terms,
  - the correct fact in one short sentence.
- Also log any questions you got right **by guessing**.
- Count errors per topic. Any topic with 2+ errors goes on your list for the weak-topics session.
- A score of 30+ out of 40 is a good sign for Extended; below 25 means knowledge gaps — check which topics.

## Quick-fire revision

- Magnification = image size ÷ actual size; 1 mm = 1000 µm.
- Osmosis: higher water potential (dilute) → lower water potential (concentrated) through a partially permeable membrane.
- Food tests: iodine blue-black; Benedict's (heat) brick-red; biuret purple; ethanol cloudy white; DCPIP decolourised.
- Enzymes: active site complementary to substrate; denatured above optimum.
- Photosynthesis 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂; respiration the reverse; yeast C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂.
- Hydrogencarbonate indicator: yellow (high CO₂), orange/red (atmospheric), purple (low CO₂).
- Xylem: water and ions (dead, lignin); phloem: sucrose and amino acids.
- Heart: left ventricle thickest; pulmonary artery carries deoxygenated blood.
- Amylase → maltose; protease → amino acids; lipase → fatty acids + glycerol; bile emulsifies.
- Inspired air 21 % O₂, 0.04 % CO₂; expired 16 % O₂, 4 % CO₂.
- Accommodation near object: ciliary muscles contract, ligaments slacken, lens fatter.
- Insulin lowers blood glucose (glucose → glycogen); glucagon raises it.
- Auxin: made in shoot tip, moves to shaded side, stimulates cell elongation.
- Tt × Tt = 3 : 1; Tt × tt = 1 : 1.
- Mitosis: identical diploid cells; meiosis: different haploid cells.
- Eutrophication: nitrate → algae → death → decomposers respire → less O₂ → fish die.
