# Biology: weak topics and definitions

> **Goal:** use your Paper 4 error log to fix your two weakest Biology topics, and make sure you can write every 0654 Biology definition in syllabus wording.

## Before you start

- Your **error log** from "Mark Paper 4" (Biology lines), plus any Biology mistakes from earlier practice.
- The Round 1 Biology pages for the topics you need.
- Blank index cards or a flashcard app; pen; timer.
- A few unused Biology questions for re-testing: the Round 1 practice questions, or Biology questions (Q1–4) from another 0654 Paper 4 or a 0654 Paper 2 you have not done.

## Exam format reminder

Biology is tested together with Chemistry and Physics in every 0654 paper:
- **Paper 2** Multiple Choice (Extended): 45 min, 40 four-option questions on all three sciences, 30 %.
- **Paper 4** Theory (Extended): 2 h, 120 marks, short-answer and structured questions, 50 % (in the specimen, Q1–4 are Biology, about 40 marks).
- **Paper 6** Alternative to Practical: 1 h 30 min, 60 marks, 20 %.
- Definitions can be asked directly ("Describe what is meant by…", "State the term…") and their key words are credited in Paper 4 mark schemes; they also help you rule out wrong options in Paper 2.

## How to do it

1. **0–8 min — sort the error log.** Count lost Biology marks by topic and by error type (K, T, C, D, M, R, S). Pick the **two topics** with the most lost marks.
2. **8–28 min — re-learn topic 1 and topic 2** (10 min each): reread only the relevant notes on the Round 1 page, close it, and write a one-side summary from memory. Check and correct in a different colour.
3. **28–45 min — flashcards.** Go through the table below. Cover the right-hand column, say or write each definition, then check every word. Make a card for each one you miss; go through the "again" pile twice more. Supplement (S) definitions are needed for Extended.
4. **45–55 min — re-test.** Answer 4–6 questions on your two weak topics. Mark strictly against the answers.
5. **55–60 min — update the log.** Date the errors you have now fixed; carry forward the rest to your next revision slot.

## Exam technique for this paper

- Definitions score through **key words**: e.g. growth needs *permanent* and *dry mass*; osmosis needs *water potential* and *partially permeable membrane*; diffusion needs *net movement* and *random movement*.
- Respiration **releases** energy — never "makes" or "produces" energy.
- For error type **T**, practise full sentences containing the key term, not just the term.
- For **C**, turn a "describe" answer into an "explain" answer by adding *because…* with a biological reason.
- For **D**, quote two values with units and the direction of change.
- Spaced repetition: review today's cards again tomorrow, in three days and in a week (well before 11 January).

## Checklist while marking

- Is each definition **complete**, with all key words — not just "roughly right"?
- Did your re-test score improve on the topics you lost most marks on in Paper 4?
- For each fixed error, write the date in the log; for each unfixed one, schedule a slot.
