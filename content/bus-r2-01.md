# Past paper · Paper 1 timed (1/2)

> **Goal:** Sit a full Paper 1 (short answer and data response) in 1 h 30 under exam conditions, without notes, so you know your real mark and timing before the mock.

## Before you start

- **Paper:** a Cambridge IGCSE Business Studies 0450 **Paper 1** (variant 11, 12 or 13) from the most recent series available — e.g. May/June or October/November 2024 or 2025 — that you have **not** done before. Papers from 2023 onwards follow the current syllabus format; if you use an older paper, skip any question on topics no longer in the syllabus.
- **Mark scheme** for the same paper and variant — keep it in another room or closed; you will use it in session 2/2.
- **Timer** set for 1 h 30 (no extra time).
- **Calculator** — allowed in both Business Studies papers. Check it works.
- Black pen, pencil, ruler; spare paper for rough working.
- Phone away, notes closed, quiet desk.

## Exam format reminder

- **Duration:** 1 hour 30 minutes. **Marks:** 80. **Weighting:** 50% of the IGCSE.
- **Four compulsory questions, 20 marks each**, each based on a short piece of stimulus material (a few lines about a business, sometimes a table or chart).
- Each question usually builds from short to long answers, typically:
  - (a) Define… [2]
  - (b) Identify / State / Calculate… [2]
  - (c) Outline… [4]
  - (d) Explain… (usually two points applied to the business) [6]
  - (e) "Do you think…? / Which option should…? Justify your answer." [6]
- The exact split can vary — always follow the marks shown in brackets.
- **Timing:** about 22 minutes per question. Rough guide: 2-mark parts ≈ 2 min, 4-mark ≈ 4–5 min, 6-mark ≈ 7–8 min.

## How to do it

| Time | What to do |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Skim all four questions. Note any stimulus data you must use. Do not start writing yet. |
| 0:03–0:25 | Question 1. Read the stimulus, underline the business name, product, size, problem. |
| 0:25–0:47 | Question 2. |
| 0:47–1:09 | Question 3. |
| 1:09–1:28 | Question 4. If behind, write the (e) decision first, then go back. |
| 1:28–1:30 | Check calculations have units and every part has an answer. |

Rules: stop exactly at 1 h 30 — draw a line under what you wrote and, if you continue, use a different colour pen so you can see what you would have scored in time. Do not check notes or the mark scheme. Spend the rest of the hour (about 25–30 minutes, or start of next session) on a quick self-review: list the questions you found hard and any terms you could not define.

## Exam technique for this paper

- **Define [2]:** learn exact definitions. Two parts usually needed (e.g. "the next best alternative" + "given up when making a choice"). Examples alone rarely get full marks.
- **Identify / State [2]:** one word or short phrase each; no explanation needed. Do not give more than asked — only the first answers are marked.
- **Calculate [2]:** write the formula, substitute, answer with units (€, %, units). The method mark can save you if the arithmetic slips.
- **Outline [4]:** typically 2 points × (identify + brief development or application).
- **Explain [6]:** usually two points, each with **knowledge** (the point), **application** (linked to the business in the stimulus), **analysis** (the consequence — on sales, costs, profit, cash, motivation). Write one paragraph per point.
- **Justify / Do you think…? [6]:** discuss both sides (or both options), apply to the business, analyse, then end with a clear decision and *why* — this final judgement is the evaluation mark. "It depends" without a decision does not score the top mark.
- **Use the stimulus:** examiners' reports often say many answers were generic. Use the business's name, product and figures in every 4- and 6-mark answer.
- Answer the question set: "advantages to the **business**" is different from "advantages to **customers**".

## Checklist while marking

(Full marking is in session 2/2 — note these now so you are ready.)
- Did each 6-mark answer have K + App + An for each point, and a justified decision in (e)?
- Did calculations show working and units?
- Did you give the number of points asked for?
- Record each lost mark in your error log as: **Q number · topic · type of error** (knowledge gap / no application / no analysis / no decision / calculation / misread command word / timing).

## Quick-fire revision

Before you start, check you can recall in under 5 minutes:
- Break-even = fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost per unit); margin of safety = actual output − break-even output.
- Gross profit margin, profit margin, ROCE, current ratio, acid test ratio formulae.
- Net cash flow = inflows − outflows; closing balance = opening balance + net cash flow.
- Labour productivity = output ÷ number of employees. Market share = firm's sales ÷ market sales × 100.
- Maslow's five levels; Herzberg's hygiene factors vs motivators.
- Job, batch, flow production; QC, QA, TQM.
- Pricing methods: cost-plus, competitive, penetration, skimming, promotional, dynamic.
- Limited vs unlimited liability; Ltd vs plc.
- SPICED: strong currency → imports cheaper, exports dearer.
- Horizontal, vertical (backward/forward), conglomerate integration.
