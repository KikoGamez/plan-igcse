# Past paper · Paper 1 timed (1/2)

> **Goal:** Complete Question 1 and Question 2 of a full 0500 Paper 1 under exam conditions in 60 minutes, so that next session you can finish Question 3.

## Before you start

- **Paper:** a Cambridge IGCSE English First Language 0500 Paper 1 (Reading) from a recent series – May/June or Oct/Nov 2024 or 2025 – that you have **not** seen. Your teacher or school can provide it; papers come with a **reading booklet insert** (Texts A, B and C). If you have none, ask your teacher for a practice paper.
- **Mark scheme:** set it aside; do not look at it until session 5 (Mark Paper 1 and Paper 2).
- **Equipment:** black pen, highlighter, a timer, a watch. No dictionary (not allowed in the exam).
- **Place:** quiet desk, phone in another room.

## Exam format reminder

- **Paper 1 Reading:** 2 hours, 80 marks, 50% of the qualification. Answer all questions.
- **Question 1 (30 marks):** short comprehension questions on Text A (15) and a summary of Text B (15: 10 reading + 5 writing, no more than 120 words, continuous prose).
- **Question 2 (25 marks):** short-answer questions on Text C (10) and the writer's effect question on two paragraphs (15).
- **Question 3 (25 marks):** extended response to Text C in a set form (15 reading + 10 writing), 250–350 words.
- Suggested full-paper timing: Q1 ≈ 45 min, Q2 ≈ 40 min, Q3 ≈ 35 min.

## How to do it

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Look over the whole paper: note the focus of the summary, the paragraphs named in Q2, the form in Q3. (This helps you read with purpose.) |
| 0:03–0:20 | Q1 short answers on Text A. One minute per mark plus reading. |
| 0:20–0:40 | Q1 summary on Text B: highlight ideas, plan, write, count words. |
| 0:40–0:60 | Q2: short answers (about 8 min), then **start** writer's effect. |

Stop exactly at 60 minutes. Draw a line under where you got to. Next session you will finish Q2 (if needed) and do Q3 in the remaining time, so the whole paper totals 2 hours. Store the paper safely – don't reread it in between.

## Exam technique for this paper

- **Mark = number of points.** 2 marks → two distinct points.
- **"Using your own words"** → change the key words; copied answers score 0 for that point.
- **"Give a word or phrase"** → quote briefly and exactly; don't copy a whole sentence.
- **"Suggest / imply"** → give the inference, not the literal fact.
- **Summary:** focus only on the stated topic; generalise examples; no introduction; never over 120 words; continuous prose.
- **Writer's effect:** short quotations from **both** named paragraphs, three from each, including imagery; meaning + effect for each; overall impression.
- **Do not overrun on Q1.** Examiners' reports often note that weaker candidates spend too long on early short questions and leave Q3 short or unfinished.

## Checklist while marking

(Marking happens in session 5, but note these now.)

- Short answers: tick only answers that match the mark scheme's **idea**; be strict on "own words".
- Summary: count how many mark-scheme ideas you included; circle lifted phrases; check word count.
- Writer's effect: highlight in two colours – meaning vs effect; check both paragraphs are covered.
- Error log: write the question type, what went wrong (time, misread question, lifting, missing effect), and one fix.

## Quick-fire revision

- R1 explicit · R2 implicit · R3 analyse/evaluate/develop · R4 writer's effects · R5 select for purpose.
- Summary: 120 words max; 10 + 5 marks; plan from notes, not the text.
- Writer's effect: 15 marks; choose 1–5 word quotations; "meaning … effect … overall".
- Q3: 250–350 words; 15 + 10 marks; address all bullets; stay in role; develop ideas.
- Own words: change key vocabulary, keep it brief.
