# Past paper · Paper 1 timed (2/2)

> **Goal:** Finish the Paper 1 you started last session – the rest of Question 2 and all of Question 3 – in the remaining 60 minutes, so you have completed a full 2-hour paper.

## Before you start

- **Paper:** the same 0500 Paper 1 and insert you began in session 1. Do not start a different paper.
- **Do not** reread your earlier answers or look at the mark scheme.
- **Equipment:** black pen, highlighter, timer. Lined paper for Q3 if needed.
- Find the line you drew where you stopped.

## Exam format reminder

- **Paper 1 Reading:** 2 hours, 80 marks.
- **Question 2 (25 marks):** short answers on Text C (10) + writer's effect on two named paragraphs (15).
- **Question 3 (25 marks: 15 reading + 10 writing):** extended response to Text C – a letter, report, journal, speech, interview or article – 250–350 words, covering every bullet point.
- Remaining time today: 60 minutes = finish Q2 (about 20 min) + Q3 (about 35 min) + check (5 min).

## Exam technique for this paper

**Writer's effect (finish)**
- If you are behind, prioritise covering the second paragraph – one paragraph alone limits the mark.
- Each choice: quote → meaning → effect of specific words. End with an overall impression.

**Question 3**
- **Read the bullets as your plan.** Typically bullet 1 is explicit content, bullet 2 asks for feelings or attitudes (inference), bullet 3 asks for views or future developments (development).
- **Use a wide range of details from Text C** but change the wording; copying lowers both the reading and writing marks.
- **Develop:** explain reasons, link details, show reactions – but stay consistent with the text; do not invent major new events.
- **Voice:** sound like the character and suit the audience (journal = private, reflective; speech = direct address; report = formal and objective).
- **Begin as instructed** if the task gives an opening.
- **Length:** 250–350 words. Much longer usually means retelling.

## Checklist while marking

(Full marking is in session 5. Today, do a 2-minute self-check after time is up.)

- Did I finish every question? If not, which one took too long, and by how much?
- Q3: put B1/B2/B3 in the margin next to each paragraph – is any bullet thin?
- Count roughly how many text details you used in Q3 and how many you **developed**.
- Error log: one line on timing, one on the weakest question.

## Quick-fire revision

- Q3 forms: letter, report, journal, speech, interview, article – know the openings and closings for each.
- 15 reading marks: range of ideas + development + all bullets + in role.
- 10 writing marks: register, structure, own words, accuracy.
- Writer's effect: both paragraphs, imagery, word-level effects, overall impression.
- Total Paper 1 time plan: Q1 ≈ 45 · Q2 ≈ 40 · Q3 ≈ 35 minutes.
