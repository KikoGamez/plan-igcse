## Exam format reminder

- **Paper 2:** 2 hours, 80 marks.
- **Section B – Composition (40 marks):** choose **one** of four titles – two descriptive, two narrative.
- **Marks:** 16 for content and structure, 24 for style and accuracy.
- **Length:** 350–450 words.
- **Time:** about 1 hour.

## Exam technique for this paper

**Descriptive**
- Create atmosphere and a vivid picture; avoid a plot.
- Use all the senses, precise nouns and verbs, and fresh imagery linked to one mood.
- Give the piece a shape: movement through space or time, contrast, a circular ending.
- Vary sentence length and openings; one-word or minor sentences can add impact.

**Narrative**
- Keep it small: 1–2 characters, one main setting, a short time.
- Start close to the action; build tension by slowing down at the key moment.
- Show character through actions and a little dialogue.
- Prepare the ending with earlier clues; avoid "it was all a dream".

**Both**
- Vocabulary should be ambitious but **accurate** – examiners reward precision over showy words.
- Stay within the word guidance: under 350 often lacks development; well over 450 often loses control.
- Leave 8–10 minutes to proofread; accuracy is a large part of the 24 style marks.

## Quick-fire revision

- Section B: 40 = 16 content/structure + 24 style/accuracy; 350–450 words.
- Descriptive toolkit: senses, precise verbs, extended metaphor, pathetic fallacy, contrast, zoom, minor sentences.
- Narrative toolkit: in medias res, want + obstacle, pace control, foreshadowing, show don't tell, prepared ending.
- Dialogue: new speaker, new line; punctuation inside speech marks.
- Proofread for: comma splices, its/it's, their/there/they're, then/than, tense consistency.
