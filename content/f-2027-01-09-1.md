# Quick Maths mock

> **Goal:** a fast, timed run through 25 mixed Extended questions covering the topics that come up most in Paper 2 and Paper 4, so you can see what is still shaky two days before the mocks.

## What to do (60 minutes)

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 0–2 min | Set up: pen, pencil, ruler, calculator *put away* for Section A |
| 2–25 min | **Section A (Paper 2 style, non-calculator)** – Questions 1–17 |
| 25–47 min | **Section B (Paper 4 style, calculator)** – Questions 18–25 |
| 47–57 min | Mark with the answers below; M marks for method, A marks for accuracy |
| 57–60 min | Error log: one row for every lost mark |

Total: **94 marks in 45 minutes.** That is faster than the real papers (about 1.2 minutes per mark), on purpose: skip anything that takes too long and come back to it. If you finish early, check every answer.

Rules: show all working, give non-exact answers to **3 significant figures** (angles to 1 decimal place) unless told otherwise, use π from your calculator in Section B.

## Section A — non-calculator (63 marks)

**1.** Write 0.000 304 7 in standard form, correct to 2 significant figures. [2]

**2.** (a) Simplify (3x²y)³. [2]
(b) Find the value of 27^(−2/3). [2]

**3.** In a sale, the price of a jacket is reduced by 20%. The sale price is €68. Find the original price. [2]

**4.** Factorise completely
(a) 6x² − 15x [1]
(b) x² − 49 [1]
(c) 2x² + 7x − 15 [2]

**5.** Solve (x + 3)/4 − (x − 2)/3 = 1. [3]

**6.** Solve the simultaneous equations
2x + 3y = 12
5x − y = 13 [3]

**7.** Solve 5 ≤ 3x + 2 < 17 and list the integer values of x that satisfy it. [3]

**8.** Make x the subject of y = (3x + 2)/(x − 1). [4]

**9.** f(x) = 2x − 3 and g(x) = x² + 1.
(a) Find fg(2). [2]
(b) Find f⁻¹(x). [2]
(c) Find gf(x), giving your answer in the form ax² + bx + c. [2]

**10.** y is inversely proportional to the square of x. When x = 2, y = 12. Find y when x = 4. [3]

**11.** A is the point (1, 4) and B is the point (5, −4).
(a) Find the gradient of AB. [2]
(b) Find the equation of the line AB in the form y = mx + c. [2]
(c) Find the coordinates of the midpoint of AB. [1]
(d) Write down the gradient of a line perpendicular to AB. [1]

**12.** y = x³ − 6x² + 9x + 1.
Find the coordinates of the two stationary points and determine whether each is a maximum or a minimum. Show your working. [6]

**13.** (a) Find the nth term of 5, 9, 13, 17, … [2]
(b) Find the nth term of 3, 8, 15, 24, … [2]
(c) Explain why 150 is not a term of the sequence in part (a). [1]

**14.** Two mathematically similar bottles have heights 12 cm and 18 cm. The smaller bottle has a volume of 400 cm³. Find the volume of the larger bottle. [3]

**15.** A bag contains 5 red counters and 3 blue counters. Two counters are taken at random, without replacement. Find the probability that both counters are the same colour. [3]

**16.** O is the origin, OA = **a** and OB = **b**. M is the midpoint of AB.
Find, in terms of **a** and **b**, in their simplest form
(a) the vector AB [1]
(b) the position vector of M. [2]

**17.** ξ = {students in a class}, n(ξ) = 30. A = {students who study French}, B = {students who study Spanish}. n(A) = 18, n(B) = 15, n(A ∩ B) = 7.
(a) Find n(A ∪ B)′. [2]
(b) Find n(A ∩ B′). [1]

## Section B — calculator (31 marks)

**18.** €5000 is invested at 3% per year compound interest. Find the value of the investment after 4 years, correct to the nearest cent. [2]

**19.** A rectangle measures 12.4 cm by 8.6 cm, each correct to the nearest 0.1 cm. Find the upper bound of the area of the rectangle. [2]

**20.** Solve 2x² + 3x − 7 = 0, giving your answers correct to 2 decimal places. [3]

**21.** In triangle ABC, angle ABC = 90°, AB = 7 cm and BC = 11 cm.
(a) Find AC. [2]
(b) Find angle BAC. [2]

**22.** In triangle PQR, PQ = 8 cm, PR = 11 cm and angle QPR = 50°.
(a) Find QR. [3]
(b) Find the area of triangle PQR. [2]

**23.** A sector of a circle has radius 6 cm and angle 75°.
(a) Find the arc length. [2]
(b) Find the area of the sector. [2]
(c) Find the perimeter of the sector. [1]

**24.** A solid cone has base radius 4 cm and perpendicular height 9 cm.
[Volume of a cone = ⅓πr²h, curved surface area of a cone = πrl, where l is the slant height.]
(a) Find the volume. [2]
(b) Find the curved surface area. [3]

**25.** The times, t minutes, that 30 students took to travel to school are shown.

| Time (t min) | 0 < t ≤ 10 | 10 < t ≤ 20 | 20 < t ≤ 30 | 30 < t ≤ 50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 4 | 9 | 12 | 5 |

(a) Calculate an estimate of the mean time. [4]
(b) On a histogram, find the frequency density for the 30 < t ≤ 50 class. [1]
