Do not learn equations that are not in 0654 (e.g. p = mv, impulse, Δp = ρgΔh, ΔE = mcΔθ, pV = constant, n = 1 ÷ sin c, E = W ÷ Q, H₀ = v ÷ d): 0654 questions never need them.

## The equations (self-test: cover the middle column)

### Motion, forces and energy

| Quantity | Equation | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | v = s ÷ t | m/s |
| Average speed | total distance ÷ total time | m/s |
| Acceleration (S) | a = Δv ÷ Δt | m/s² |
| Speed–time graph | distance = area under the graph; acceleration = gradient (S) | m; m/s² |
| Distance–time graph | speed = gradient | m/s |
| Weight | W = mg (g = W ÷ m) | N (m in kg, g in N/kg) |
| Density | ρ = m ÷ V | kg/m³ or g/cm³ |
| Force, mass, acceleration (S) | F = ma | N |
| Spring constant (S) | k = F ÷ x | N/m or N/cm (x = extension) |
| Moment | moment = force × perpendicular distance from the pivot | N m |
| Principle of moments (S) | clockwise moment = anticlockwise moment | N m |
| Kinetic energy (S) | Eₖ = ½mv² | J |
| Change in gravitational potential energy (S) | ΔEₚ = mgΔh | J |
| Work done | W = Fd = ΔE | J |
| Power | P = W ÷ t = ΔE ÷ t | W |
| Efficiency (S) | (useful energy output ÷ total energy input) × 100% = (useful power output ÷ total power input) × 100% | % |
| Pressure | p = F ÷ A | Pa (N/m²) or N/cm² |

### Thermal physics

No equations in 0654 — only particle and energy-transfer explanations. Water melts at 0 °C and boils at 100 °C (standard atmospheric pressure).

### Waves

| Quantity | Equation | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Wave speed | v = fλ | m/s (f in Hz, λ in m) |
| Reflection | angle of incidence = angle of reflection | degrees, from the normal |
| Refractive index (S) | n = sin i ÷ sin r; also n = ratio of the speeds of a wave in two regions | no unit |
| Echo | distance to reflector = speed × time ÷ 2 | m |

Also know: speed of EM waves in a vacuum = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s (S); audible range 20 Hz – 20 kHz; ultrasound above 20 kHz.

### Electricity and magnetism

|---|---|---|

### Nuclear physics

| Quantity | Equation / rule | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Neutrons in a nucleus | number of neutrons = A − Z | A nucleon number, Z proton number |
| Half-life | after n half-lives: remaining = original × (½)ⁿ | no background correction in 0654 |
| Decay equations (S) | α: A − 4, Z − 2; β: A unchanged, Z + 1; γ: no change | top and bottom numbers balance |

### Space physics

| Quantity | Equation | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Light travel time | t = d ÷ c, c = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s | s |
| Orbital speed (S) | v = 2πr ÷ T | m/s (r = orbit radius, T = period) |

Also know: 1 light-year = distance travelled by light in a vacuum in one year (≈ 9.5 × 10¹⁵ m) — a distance, not a time.

## Quick-fire calculations

1. A 2.0 kW heater is used for 3 hours. Electricity costs 30 cents per kW h. Find the energy used in kW h and the cost.
2. Find the kinetic energy of a 1200 kg car moving at 20 m/s.
3. A transformer has 2000 primary turns and 100 secondary turns. The primary voltage is 230 V. Find the secondary voltage.
4. A force of 600 N acts on an area of 0.030 m². Find the pressure.
5. Light enters glass with angle of incidence 40° and angle of refraction 25°. Find the refractive index.
6. A radioactive sample has a count rate of 800 counts/min and a half-life of 2 hours. Find the count rate after 6 hours.
7. Find the combined resistance of 6.0 Ω and 3.0 Ω in parallel.
8. A spring extends by 0.040 m when a force of 2.0 N is applied. Find the spring constant.

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. 2.0 × 3 = **6.0 kW h**; cost = 6.0 × 30 = **180 cents**
2. Eₖ = ½ × 1200 × 20² = **240 000 J**
3. Vₛ = 230 × 100 ÷ 2000 = **11.5 V**
4. p = 600 ÷ 0.030 = **20 000 Pa**
5. n = sin 40° ÷ sin 25° = 0.643 ÷ 0.423 = **1.52**
6. 6 h = 3 half-lives; 800 × (½)³ = **100 counts/min**
7. 1/R = 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/2, R = **2.0 Ω**
8. k = 2.0 ÷ 0.040 = **50 N/m**

</details>

## Error-log routine for Physics

For each unticked Physics row: cover the fix, redo the original question, then check. Typical Physics fixes to add to your sheet:
- Convert units **first**: g → kg, cm³ → m³, kJ → J, minutes → s, cm² → m² (but kW h uses kW × **hours**).
- Echo questions: the sound travels **there and back** — halve the distance.
- Round only at the end; give 2–3 significant figures and the **unit**.
- "Explain" questions on energy: name the **stores** and the **transfer pathway**.
- Moments: distance must be **perpendicular** from the pivot to the line of the force.
