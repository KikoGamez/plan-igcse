## The equations (self-test: cover the middle column)

### Motion, forces and energy

| Quantity | Equation | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | v = s ÷ t | m/s |
| Average speed | total distance ÷ total time | m/s |
| Acceleration | a = Δv ÷ Δt | m/s² |
| Distance from a speed–time graph | area under the graph | m |
| Weight | W = mg | N (m in kg, g in N/kg) |
| Gravitational field strength | g = W ÷ m | N/kg |
| Density | ρ = m ÷ V | kg/m³ or g/cm³ |
| Spring constant (Hooke's law) | k = F ÷ x | N/m (x = extension) |
| Force, mass, acceleration | F = ma | N |
| Moment | moment = force × perpendicular distance from the pivot | N m |
| Principle of moments (equilibrium) | sum of clockwise moments = sum of anticlockwise moments, and resultant force = 0 | |
| Momentum | p = mv | kg m/s |
| Impulse | impulse = FΔt = Δ(mv) | N s |
| Resultant force | F = Δp ÷ Δt (rate of change of momentum) | N |
| Kinetic energy | Eₖ = ½mv² | J |
| Change in gravitational potential energy | ΔEₚ = mgΔh | J |
| Work done | W = Fd = ΔE | J |
| Power | P = W ÷ t = ΔE ÷ t | W |
| Efficiency | (useful energy output ÷ total energy input) × 100% = (useful power output ÷ total power input) × 100% | % |
| Pressure | p = F ÷ A | Pa (N/m²) |
| Pressure change in a liquid | Δp = ρgΔh | Pa |

### Thermal physics

| Quantity | Equation | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Kelvin and Celsius | T (in K) = θ (in °C) + 273 | K |
| Gas at constant temperature (fixed mass) | pV = constant, so p₁V₁ = p₂V₂ | any consistent units |
| Specific heat capacity | c = ΔE ÷ (mΔθ), so ΔE = mcΔθ | J/(kg °C) |

### Waves

| Quantity | Equation | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Wave speed | v = fλ | m/s (f in Hz, λ in m) |
| Refractive index | n = sin i ÷ sin r | no unit |
| Critical angle | n = 1 ÷ sin c | no unit |

### Electricity and magnetism

| Quantity | Equation | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Current | I = Q ÷ t | A (Q in C) |
| Electromotive force / potential difference | E = W ÷ Q, V = W ÷ Q | V (W = energy in J) |
| Resistance | R = V ÷ I | Ω |
| Resistance of a wire | R ∝ length, R ∝ 1 ÷ cross-sectional area | |
| Resistors in series | R = R₁ + R₂ + … | Ω |
| Resistors in parallel | 1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + … | Ω |
| Series circuit | current the same everywhere; e.m.f. = sum of p.d.s | |
| Parallel circuit | total current = sum of branch currents | |
| Potential divider | V₁ ÷ V₂ = R₁ ÷ R₂ | |
| Electrical power | P = IV | W |
| Electrical energy | E = IVt | J (or kWh: kW × h) |
| Transformer turns | Vₚ ÷ Vₛ = Nₚ ÷ Nₛ | |
| Transformer power (100% efficient) | IₚVₚ = IₛVₛ | W |
| Power loss in cables | P = I²R | W |

### Space physics

| Quantity | Equation | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Orbital speed | v = 2πr ÷ T | m/s (r = orbit radius, T = period) |
| Hubble constant | H₀ = v ÷ d | per second (s⁻¹) |
| Age of the Universe estimate | d ÷ v = 1 ÷ H₀ | s |

Also know: 1 light-year = distance travelled by light in a vacuum in one year; speed of light in a vacuum = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s.

## Quick-fire calculations

1. Find the energy needed to heat 2.0 kg of water by 15 °C. (c = 4200 J/(kg °C))
2. Find the kinetic energy of a 1200 kg car moving at 20 m/s.
3. A transformer has 2000 primary turns and 100 secondary turns. The primary voltage is 230 V. Find the secondary voltage.
4. Find the pressure due to 0.80 m of water. (ρ = 1000 kg/m³)
5. Light enters glass with angle of incidence 40° and angle of refraction 25°. Find the refractive index.
6. Find the critical angle of a material with refractive index 1.5.
7. Find the combined resistance of 6.0 Ω and 3.0 Ω in parallel.
8. A gas at 100 kPa has volume 300 cm³. It is compressed at constant temperature to 120 cm³. Find the new pressure.

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. ΔE = mcΔθ = 2.0 × 4200 × 15 = **126 000 J**
2. Eₖ = ½ × 1200 × 20² = **240 000 J**
3. Vₛ = 230 × 100 ÷ 2000 = **11.5 V**
4. Δp = 1000 × 9.8 × 0.80 = **7840 Pa**
5. n = sin 40° ÷ sin 25° = 0.643 ÷ 0.423 = **1.52**
6. sin c = 1 ÷ 1.5 = 0.667, c = **41.8°**
7. 1/R = 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/2, R = **2.0 Ω**
8. p₂ = 100 × 300 ÷ 120 = **250 kPa**

</details>

## Error-log routine for Physics

For each unticked Physics row: cover the fix, redo the original question, then check. Typical Physics fixes to add to your sheet:
- Convert units **first**: g → kg, cm³ → m³, kJ → J, minutes → s, cm² → m².
- Use Δθ (temperature **change**), not a final temperature.
- Round only at the end; give 2–3 significant figures and the **unit**.
- "Explain" questions on energy: name the **stores** and the **transfer pathway**.
- Moments: distance must be **perpendicular** from the pivot to the line of the force.
