## Formulae to know

### Number and algebra
- Percentage change = (change ÷ original) × 100
- Reverse percentage: original = new value ÷ multiplier (e.g. after 20% off, ÷ 0.8)
- Compound interest / growth: value = P(1 + r/100)ⁿ; depreciation: P(1 − r/100)ⁿ
- Simple interest: I = PRT ÷ 100
- Index laws: aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ; aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ; (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ; a⁰ = 1; a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ; a^(m/n) = (ⁿ√a)ᵐ
- Quadratic formula: x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a
- Completing the square: x² + bx + c = (x + b/2)² − (b/2)² + c
- Difference of two squares: a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)
- nth term: linear an + b; quadratic an² + bn + c (a = half the second difference)
- Direct proportion y = kx (or kx², k√x); inverse y = k/x (or k/x²)
- Speed = distance ÷ time; density = mass ÷ volume; pressure = force ÷ area

### Coordinate geometry and calculus
- Gradient m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁); line y = mx + c
- Midpoint ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2); length √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²)
- Perpendicular gradients: m₁ × m₂ = −1
- Differentiation: y = axⁿ → dy/dx = naxⁿ⁻¹; stationary points where dy/dx = 0; d²y/dx² < 0 maximum, > 0 minimum

### Geometry and mensuration
- Pythagoras: a² + b² = c²; SOH CAH TOA
- Sine rule: a/sin A = b/sin B = c/sin C
- Cosine rule: a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos A
- Area of triangle = ½ab sin C
- Circle: circumference 2πr, area πr²; arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr; sector area = (θ/360) × πr²
- Trapezium area = ½(a + b)h; parallelogram = bh
- Prism / cylinder volume = cross-section area × length; cylinder πr²h, curved surface 2πrh
- Cone: V = ⅓πr²h, curved surface πrl; sphere: V = (4/3)πr³, surface 4πr²; pyramid: V = ⅓ × base area × h
- Similar shapes, scale factor k: lengths × k, areas × k², volumes × k³
- Interior angles of an n-sided polygon sum to (n − 2) × 180°; exterior angles sum to 360°
- Circle theorems: angle in a semicircle 90°; angle at centre = 2 × angle at circumference; angles in the same segment equal; opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add to 180°; tangent ⟂ radius; tangents from a point equal; alternate segment theorem

### Statistics and probability
- Mean from grouped data = Σfx ÷ Σf (x = midpoint)
- Frequency density = frequency ÷ class width
- P(not A) = 1 − P(A); P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) for mutually exclusive events; P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B) for independent events
- Without replacement: second fraction changes (numerator and denominator)
- Cumulative frequency: median at n/2, quartiles at n/4 and 3n/4; IQR = UQ − LQ

## Common mistakes (what loses marks)

- **Accuracy:** final answers to 3 significant figures (angles 1 d.p.) unless told otherwise; **never round in the middle** — keep full values on the calculator.
- **Premature rounding in trigonometry** leading to an answer outside the mark scheme's range.
- **Units and area/volume scale factors:** using k instead of k² or k³; mixing cm and m.
- **Negative signs:** −4 × −2 = +8 when expanding; subtracting a bracket (x − 2).
- **Inequalities:** forgetting to reverse the sign when multiplying or dividing by a negative.
- **Bounds:** upper bound of a difference = upper − lower; of a quotient = upper ÷ lower.
- **Probability without replacement:** not reducing the denominator.
- **Histograms:** plotting frequency instead of frequency density.
- **Cumulative frequency:** plotting at midpoints instead of upper class boundaries.
- **Function notation:** fg(x) means apply **g first**; f⁻¹(x) is the inverse, not 1/f(x).
- **Show working:** "show that" questions need every step; a correct answer with no working may get no marks.
- **Non-calculator paper:** check the arithmetic by an estimate; leave answers as exact fractions or surds when asked.

## Exam technique reminders

- Read the marks: 1 mark = a quick answer; 4+ marks = several clear steps.
- Write the formula, substitute, then calculate — method marks come even if the final answer is wrong.
- If stuck after 2 minutes, move on and mark the question to come back to.
- Use the last 10 minutes to check: answers to the right accuracy, units, every part answered.
