# Past paper · Reading + marking

> **Goal:** Do one complete 0520 Reading paper in exam conditions, then mark it with the mark scheme and log every error by cause.

## Before you start

- **Paper:** a recent 0520 Reading paper (May/June or Oct/Nov 2024 or 2025) you have **not** done, plus the matching mark scheme (same variant number). Ask your teacher if you don't have access — papers are on the Cambridge School Support Hub.
- Black pen, timer, error log. **No dictionary.**
- Phone in another room. Mark scheme closed until the timer stops.

## Exam format reminder

- Check the front cover for the exact duration and total marks. The time available is less than the full hour of this session, so you have time to mark.
- Exercises progress from short texts (signs, notices, short messages, adverts) to longer texts (emails, blogs, articles, interviews) on topics A–E.
- Question types: matching (texts ↔ people, headings ↔ paragraphs), multiple choice, true/false (sometimes with correction of false statements) and short answers in French.
- Rough timing guide: spend less time on the early exercises (they're shorter and easier) and save at least half your time for the last two or three longer texts, which carry many marks.

## How to do it

1. **Before starting** — read the cover; note the time allowed; write your target finish time at the top.
2. **Exam time** — do the paper in order. If a question blocks you for more than a minute, mark it with a star and move on.
3. **Last 5 minutes of the exam time** — go back to starred questions; fill every blank with your best guess; check answers are in French where required.
4. **Remaining time** — mark with the mark scheme (aim for 10–15 minutes) and fill in the error log.

## Exam technique for this paper

- **Questions first, then text.** Underline key words in each question, then scan the text for them or their synonyms.
- **Questions follow text order** within an exercise — use that to locate answers.
- **Synonyms and paraphrase:** the question will say *Pourquoi Marie est-elle contente ?* while the text says *Marie était ravie parce que…*. Expect *commencer/débuter, aimer/adorer/se passionner pour, cher/coûteux, travailler/bosser, rapide/vite*.
- **Matching:** read all the descriptions of the people first. Look for the detail that **excludes** an option (*sauf le dimanche*, *pas pour les débutants*). One option is often a near-miss.
- **True/false:** the statement must be **supported by the text**. Watch for absolutes (*toujours, jamais, tous, seulement*) and for changes of plan.
- **Short answers in French:** lift only the relevant words. A brief phrase is usually enough (*parce qu'il pleuvait*, *un maillot de bain*). Extra information that contradicts the answer loses the mark.
- **Grammar clues:** if the question asks *Qu'est-ce qu'il a fait ?*, the answer is an action; *Comment était… ?* wants a description/adjective.
- **Don't panic over unknown words:** use cognates, Spanish, context and word families.

## Checklist while marking

- Use a different colour. Apply the mark scheme strictly: if the scheme says a key idea is needed (e.g. *trop cher*), check your answer has it.
- Look at the "accept / reject" guidance. Note which kinds of answers were rejected — they show you how examiners think.
- For each lost mark, record in the error log: paper, question, your answer, correct answer, cause:
  - **V** — key word unknown (add to Anki)
  - **P** — missed a paraphrase/synonym
  - **D** — distractor or change of plan
  - **L** — lifted too much / too little
  - **Q** — misread the question (who/when/why)
  - **T** — ran out of time
- Total per exercise and as a percentage. If you lose most marks in the long texts, next time move faster through the early ones.
- Reread the text for every wrong answer and underline where the answer was. Don't skip this step — it's where you learn.

## Quick-fire revision

- Question words: *qui, que, quoi, où, quand, pourquoi, comment, combien, quel(le)(s), lequel*.
- Instruction words: *cochez, écrivez la lettre, répondez en français, vrai/faux, corrigez, complétez, reliez* (match).
- Negatives that flip meaning: *ne… pas / jamais / plus / rien / personne / que (only) / aucun(e) (no, not any)*.
- Connectives that signal the answer: *mais, cependant, pourtant, par contre, donc, alors, grâce à (thanks to), à cause de (because of), malgré (despite)*.
- Common false friends: *la librairie* (bookshop), *le car* (coach), *rester* (stay), *la location* (rental), *actuellement* (currently), *le stage* (work placement), *sensible* (sensitive).
- Time expressions: *il y a deux ans* (two years ago), *depuis* (since/for), *pendant* (during/for), *dans une semaine* (in a week's time), *autrefois* (in the past), *désormais* (from now on).
