## Exam technique for this paper

- **Section B evaluate-presentation (8 marks):** for each method (e.g. bar chart, map, annotated photo) give **strength** (clear comparison, shows pattern) and **weakness** (missing scale, too few sites, no key, cannot show change, bias), say whether it suits the enquiry aim, and finish with an overall judgement and one improvement. Refer to figures by name (Figure 4a).
- **Section C 6-mark "Assess":** 3 factors, each explained and linked to the resource; then say which is most important and why.
- **12-mark "Discuss the view that ...":** (1) one sentence: what the statement means; (2) **evidence for** with data from the figures and an example; (3) **evidence against** with a contrasting example; (4) a **conclusion** with a reasoned judgement ("To a large extent ... because ..., although ..."). Use both figures and your own knowledge. Aim for at least 5 developed points.
- **"Suggest two reasons for the pattern":** describe the pattern first (high in ..., low in ...), then give two reasons for the difference (e.g. level of development, trade, history, population).
- **Calculations:** write "highest − lowest = ..." then answer with units.
- **"Describe a trend":** direction, data (two figures with years), any anomaly.
- **Named examples** matter: use real examples (e.g. Amazon rainforest, Sahel, TNC like Nike or Apple, migration from Syria, aid programmes, bottom-up water project).
- Do not spend more than 15 minutes on the 12-mark question.

## Quick-fire revision

- Fragile environments: deserts, rainforests, polar and mountain areas, coral reefs. Desertification causes: drought, overgrazing, population pressure, fuel wood. Impacts: crop failure, famine, migration.
- Deforestation causes: logging, agriculture, mining, roads, settlement, HEP; impacts: loss of biodiversity, climate change, soil erosion.
- Natural climate change: Milankovitch cycles, solar variation, volcanism; enhanced greenhouse effect: burning fossil fuels, farming, transport, industry.
- Effects of climate change: rising sea level, more hazards, ecosystem change, health and food supply.
- Globalisation drivers: trade, investment, aid, labour, transport, IT; institutions: WTO (rules on trade), IMF (loans).
- TNC benefits: jobs, tax, skills; costs: profit leaves, low wages, environment.
- Migration: push/pull; voluntary/forced; remittances; brain drain; tourism: multiplier effect, leakage, ecotourism.
- Development measures: GDP per capita, HDI, inequality, corruption indices; uneven development: poverty, unemployment, housing.
- Demographic data: birth and death rate, natural increase, fertility, infant mortality (deaths under 1 per 1,000 live births per year).
- Strategies: aid, intergovernmental agreements; top-down (large, government) v bottom-up (small, local, sustainable) projects.
- Range = highest − lowest; always write the working.
