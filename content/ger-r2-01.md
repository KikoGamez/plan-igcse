# Past paper · Listening + marking

> **Goal:** Do one complete Cambridge IGCSE German 0525 Paper 1 Listening with its audio under exam conditions, mark it with the mark scheme and log every error by cause.

## Before you start

- **Paper:** a recent Paper 1 Listening that you have **not** done: the specimen paper for the 2025–2027 syllabus, or May/June or Oct/Nov 2025 (Paper 11, 12 or 13; the number is the variant and it must match the audio and the mark scheme). Papers from before 2025 may be laid out differently, so check that yours has 37 questions. Your teacher can download the question paper, **audio**, transcript and mark scheme from the Cambridge School Support Hub.
- Ready: headphones or speaker, a quiet room, pencil/pen, the **answer sheet** (or a plain sheet with numbers 1–37), a timer, an error log.
- Keep the transcript and mark scheme closed until you have finished.
- Time needed: **about 50 minutes** (the audio includes transfer time), then marking.

## Exam format reminder

- **Paper 1: about 50 minutes including 6 minutes' transfer time, 40 marks, 37 multiple-choice and matching questions.** You hear each recording twice. All answers are options (letters), so no German writing.
- **Q1–8** (8 marks): short texts, 4 options. **Q9–14** (6): factual monologue or dialogue, 4 options. **Q15–19** (5): informal conversation, matching. **Q20–28** (9): conversation/interview, 3 options. **Q29–34** (6): conversation, 4 options. **Q35–37** (6): choose the **two true statements** from five.
- Topics from areas A–E. Questions get harder: the last 21 marks are Q20–37.

## How to do it

1. **0–2 min:** Set up. Put the mark scheme away. Check you have the right paper and audio.
2. **2–52 min:** Play the audio straight through **without pausing or rewinding**. Use the reading time for each section: read all questions and options; underline the key word; predict the answer. During the first listening tick a likely answer; during the second listening confirm or change. During the **6-minute transfer time**, copy each answer to the answer sheet and check every one.
3. **52–60 min:** Open the mark scheme and the transcript. Mark. Count marks per section. Then, for **each wrong answer**, find the exact sentence in the transcript. Write one line in the error log:
   - **Q# · what I answered · what was right · cause** (number trap, signal word missed, vocabulary, ran out of time, transfer error, guessed).
4. Last 3 minutes: choose one fix for next time (e.g. "write digits at once", "wait for *aber*").

## Exam technique for this paper

- **Use every second of reading time.** Underline the question word and the key word in each option (*Wann? Wo? Wie viel?*).
- **Predict.** A price question: expect a number. A reason question: expect *weil, denn, deshalb*.
- **Signal words:** *aber, leider, eigentlich, stattdessen, sondern, nicht mehr, früher/jetzt, außer, dann doch.* The final answer often follows them.
- **Numbers and times:** write the digits as soon as you hear them. *dreizehn/dreißig, sechzehn/sechzig, siebzehn/siebzig.* *zweiundzwanzig* = 22. *halb acht* = 7.30.
- **Q15–19 matching:** each item is used once; a speaker can mention several things but **likes only one**.
- **Q35–37 (two true):** tick exactly two. Reject options that change a detail (*früher* instead of *jetzt*, *nie* instead of *selten*).
- **Never leave a blank.** No marks are lost for a wrong answer.
- **Transfer carefully:** the right answer in the wrong box scores 0.

## Checklist while marking

- Did I leave any blank? (Guess next time.)
- Marks per section: Q1–8 / 8 · Q9–14 / 6 · Q15–19 / 5 · Q20–28 / 9 · Q29–34 / 6 · Q35–37 / 6. Which section lost most?
- For each wrong answer: did I **hear** the right information but choose wrongly (a technique problem), or **not understand** it (a vocabulary problem)?
- Transfer errors: any answers on the paper that don't match my sheet?
- Which signal word or number did I miss? Write it in the log.
- Total: ___ / 40. Target: add 3 marks by fixing the most common cause.
- Learn **ten words** from the transcript that you did not know (with *der/die/das*).

## Quick-fire revision

- **Listening = 40 marks, 37 questions, MCQ and matching, texts played twice, transfer time at the end.**
- Q35–37: pick **two** of five.
- *nicht … sondern* = not … but: the second one is right.
- *seit zwei Jahren* = for two years (still true); *vor zwei Jahren* = two years ago.
- *Viertel nach drei* = 3.15; *Viertel vor vier* = 3.45; *halb vier* = 3.30.
- *Hin- und Rückfahrt* = return trip; *einfache Fahrt* = single.
- *kostenlos / gratis / umsonst* = free; *ermäßigt* = reduced.
- *Gleis* = platform; *Verspätung* = delay; *Haltestelle* = stop.
- Weather: *es regnet, schneit, ist windig, neblig, bewölkt, sonnig*.
- Health: *Ich habe Fieber / Husten / Kopfschmerzen*.
- Opinions: *Ich finde es toll / langweilig / anstrengend / nützlich*.
- Jobs: *Arzt, Lehrer, Verkäufer, Koch, Ingenieur, Polizist*.
- Reasons: *weil, denn, deshalb, darum*.
