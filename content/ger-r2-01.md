# Past paper · Listening + marking

> **Goal:** Do one full Pearson Edexcel International GCSE German Paper 1 (4GN1/01) under exam conditions with the real audio, mark it with the mark scheme, and turn every lost mark into a fix.

## Before you start

- **Which paper:** a Pearson Edexcel **International GCSE German (4GN1) Paper 1 Listening**, from the past papers page on the Pearson qualifications website (search "International GCSE German 2017 past papers"). Download the **question paper, the audio (MP3) and the mark scheme** (with the transcript, if given) for the **same series**. Use a recent series (for example Summer 2023, 2024 or 2025) and a different series for any later repeat. Ask your teacher if the audio is not on the website: they have it.
- If you have already done the paper in "Listening: practice", use another series.
- Have ready: black pen, **a second pen colour for marking**, the printed question paper, headphones or speakers, a timer or phone for the timer, a clock, and no dictionary.
- Switch off your phone notifications. Sit at a desk. Do not pause the audio.
- Keep the mark scheme and transcript **face down** until the marking stage.

## Exam format reminder

- **Paper 1 Listening (4GN1/01):** 30 minutes plus **5 minutes' reading time**, **40 marks**, **25%** of the qualification. Seven questions (in the sample: Q1 4 marks, Q2–Q7 6 marks each).
- Each recording is played **twice** with a pause between questions. You may write at any time. Tasks vary (multiple choice, short answers, grids, matching); **read the instruction on each question to see what language to answer in and how many answers to give**.
- All five themes can appear: Home and abroad; Education and employment; Personal life and relationships; The world around us; Social activities, fitness and health.
- No dictionary. No marks are lost for wrong answers.

## How to do it

1. **0–3 min:** set up (paper, pens, timer, audio ready). Read the "Exam technique" section below once.
2. **3–8 min (reading time):** the real 5 minutes. Read every question, underline question words and mark counts, and predict vocabulary. Write nothing on the audio yet.
3. **8–38 min:** play the audio **without stopping**. First playing: gist and numbers. Second playing: check and complete. Use the pauses to write, not to daydream.
4. **38–40 min:** last check of every question: no blanks, answers in the right language.
5. **40–55 min:** mark in a **different colour**. For each question: tick correct, cross wrong, and write the model answer in the margin. Use the mark scheme exactly: note what answers are **accepted** and what is **not** (e.g. too vague).
6. **55–60 min:** read the **transcript** for the questions you lost marks on, listening again if you can. Write an error log (see checklist). Total your mark out of 40 and note the grade you would need to improve.

## Exam technique for this paper

- Use the 5 minutes: topic, answer language, marks, predicted words.
- **First listening for numbers and names; second for detail and checking.**
- Don't pick the first thing you hear: watch **aber, sondern, eigentlich, leider, früher/jetzt**.
- Numbers: *halb acht* = 7.30; *dreizehn/dreißig*, *sechzehn/sechzig* stress and units-first (*vierundzwanzig* = 24).
- **Negatives:** *kein, nie, nicht, niemand*.
- Give **specific details** (e.g. "sunny and hot", not "good weather"). One idea per mark.
- Never leave a gap: **guess** from the options or the context.
- If you lose the thread, **move on** and use the second playing.
- Check you used the **language and format** the question asks for (letters, ticks, English or German).
- Spell numbers and names clearly (write digits for numbers where allowed).

## Checklist while marking

- [ ] Used the mark scheme's **accepted answers**, not what "sounds right".
- [ ] For each lost mark, noted **why**: ① didn't know the word, ② number/time slip, ③ distractor (picked the first mention), ④ too vague, ⑤ misread the question, ⑥ wrote in the wrong language, ⑦ ran out of time.
- [ ] Wrote the **missed German words** (with the article) into a vocabulary list.
- [ ] Listened again to the 2 hardest items with the transcript.
- [ ] Counted how many marks came from numbers/times: practise those if more than 2.
- [ ] Total mark: ____ / 40. Target for next time: +4.
- [ ] Decide: which one skill will I practise tomorrow (numbers, opinion words, past/future markers)?

## Quick-fire revision

- *halb sieben* = 6.30; *Viertel vor acht* = 7.45; *Viertel nach drei* = 3.15.
- *dreizehn* 13, *dreißig* 30, *vierzehn* 14, *vierzig* 40, *sechzehn* 16, *sechzig* 60, *siebzehn* 17, *siebzig* 70.
- *einundzwanzig* = 21 (units first).
- *am ersten / dritten / zwanzigsten* = dates: 1st / 3rd / 20th.
- *aber* and *sondern* introduce the real answer; *leider* = bad news follows.
- *früher/damals* = past; *jetzt/heutzutage* = present; *nächstes Jahr/in Zukunft* = future.
- *kein / nie / niemals* = no / never.
- *weil / denn / deshalb* introduce reasons.
- *der Müll* rubbish; *die Verspätung* delay; *das Gleis* platform; *der Stau* traffic jam.
- *Wie lange?* how long; *Wie viel?* how much; *Wie viele?* how many; *Wann?* when; *Wer?* who.
- *das Praktikum* work experience; *die Ausbildung* apprenticeship; *die Klassenfahrt* school trip.
- *Ich habe Kopfschmerzen / Fieber / eine Erkältung.* illness vocabulary.
- *am liebsten* = best of all.
- 1 mark = 1 piece of information; write the detail, not a general word.
