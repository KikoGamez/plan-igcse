## How to do it

1. **0:00 – 0:02** Re-read the question where you stopped, including the information at the start of that question.
2. **0:02 – 0:47** Continue through the remaining questions. Read the stem of each question carefully — information given at the start is often needed in part (d) or (e).
3. **0:47 – 0:52** Return to starred parts.
4. **0:52 – 1:00** Final check:
   - every part attempted and answered on the answer line;
   - accuracy: 3 s.f. (or as stated), money to 2 d.p., angles 1 d.p.;
   - units match the answer line;
   - sense check: lengths positive, probabilities between 0 and 1, an angle in a triangle less than 180°, a speed that is realistic.
5. Stop at 1:00. Write the total time used and any unfinished questions on the cover.

## Exam technique for this paper

Later Paper 4 questions often cover these areas — technique for each:

- **Mensuration (areas, volumes, sectors):** write the formula, substitute, keep π in the calculator. Convert units before calculating (1 m³ = 1 000 000 cm³; 1 litre = 1000 cm³).
- **Similar shapes:** length factor k → area factor k², volume factor k³.
- **3D trigonometry:** draw the right-angled triangle you need separately; the angle between a line and a plane is in a triangle with the line's projection on the plane.
- **Sine and cosine rules:** cosine rule for an angle when all three sides are known; sine rule when you have an opposite pair. Area = ½ab sin C.
- **Vectors and transformations:** describe transformations fully (e.g. "rotation, 90° anticlockwise, centre (1, 2)"); a single transformation means one word only.
- **Probability trees:** multiply along branches, add between outcomes; "at least one" = 1 − P(none).
- **Statistics:** histograms use frequency density = frequency ÷ class width; read medians and quartiles from the cumulative frequency graph at n/2, n/4, 3n/4.
- **Functions and graphs:** see the Paper 2 advice; on Paper 4 you may be asked to draw a tangent and estimate a gradient — use two points far apart on your tangent.
- **Running out of time:** a method mark is worth having. Write the first step of the method even if you cannot finish.

## Quick-fire revision

Formulae to have ready in your head (not all of these are on the formula list):
- Pythagoras: a² + b² = c²;
- SOH CAH TOA;
- arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr; sector area = (θ/360) × πr²;
- compound interest: P(1 + r/100)ⁿ;
- speed = distance ÷ time; density = mass ÷ volume;
- percentage change = change ÷ original × 100;
- estimated mean = Σfx ÷ Σf;
- frequency density = frequency ÷ class width.
