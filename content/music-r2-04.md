# Set works and vocabulary: weak points

> **Goal:** Use your error log to fix the weakest content: the focus works (Vivaldi, Handel, Mozart, Smetana), the starting-point pieces for Areas 4–7, and the vocabulary list. Finish with a one-page fact sheet and a test of 15 terms.

## Before you start

- Your **error log** from the last session, a **blank page** for each fact sheet, and the **syllabus vocabulary list** (Paper 1 content table). A teacher's list of set works for this year if you have one: **check your set-work list**.
- Recordings of the focus works: Vivaldi *Spring* (1st movement), Handel Concerto Grosso Op. 6 No. 5 (movements 1, 2, 4, 6), Mozart K. 452 (1st movement), Smetana *Vltava*.
- Index cards or the app's flash cards for terms. Timer for 60 minutes.

## Exam format reminder

- **Paper 1 Listening:** c.1 h 15 min, 70 marks, 40% of the grade; eight extracts, at least one from each of the seven areas.
- **Areas 1–3** (Baroque, Classical, Romantic) use **focus works**; the exam never plays them, but extracts are related to them in **structure, genre or style**. Use them as models for the style.
- **Areas 4–7** (music and words, dance, small ensemble, stage and screen) use **starting points** (not prescribed).
- Questions can ask you to identify, describe, compare, follow a score, name keys, intervals, chords and cadences, transpose, and identify structure.
- Coursework (performing and composing) is separate: 30% each.

## Exam technique for this paper

- **Learn by function, not by memory:** each fact must answer "what feature does it show?" (e.g. "ritornello = Baroque concerto").
- **Use focus works as a model:** when you hear a concerto in the exam, think "ritornello, concertino/ripieno, continuo"; for a chamber piece, "sonata form, conversational"; for orchestral programme music, "leitmotif/theme, orchestral colours".
- **Do not write set-work facts** for an unfamiliar extract: talk only about what you hear.
- **Vocabulary:** write definitions in your own words that match **exam wording**; test on cards one-way (term → definition) and the other way.
- **Transposition and score reading:** repeat five quick tasks: key from signature, interval names, one cadence, one transposition, one structure letter pattern.
- **Spaced repetition:** retest tomorrow, in three days and in one week.

## Quick-fire revision

- Vivaldi *Spring* mvt 1: solo concerto, ritornello form, E major, programme music.
- Handel Op. 6 No. 5: concerto grosso, D major, concertino 2 violins + cello, continuo.
- Mozart K. 452: piano + oboe, clarinet, horn, bassoon; E flat; Largo then Allegro moderato; sonata form.
- Smetana *Vltava*: symphonic poem; second of six in *Má vlast*; E minor then E major; river theme.
- Sonata form: exposition, development, recapitulation; second subject dominant then tonic.
- Baroque: terraced dynamics, continuo; Classical: Alberti bass, balanced phrases.
- Romantic: large orchestra, rubato, chromatic harmony, nationalism.
- Salsa: clave, congas, timbales, brass, call and response.
- Tango: bandoneón, marcado, habanera, Piazzolla nuevo tango.
- Hindustani: sitar, tabla, tanpura; rāga, tāla; ālāp, jōr, jhālā, gat.
- Arab takht: oud, qanun, nay, violin, riqq, darbuka; maqām and quarter tones.
- Silk and bamboo: erhu, pipa, guzheng, yangqin, dizi; heterophonic, pentatonic.
- EDM: synthesiser, drum machine, sample, loop, build up, drop, breakdown.
- Film: leitmotif, fanfare, tremolo, ostinato, dissonance.
