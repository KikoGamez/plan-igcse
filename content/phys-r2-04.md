# Physics: weak topics and equations

> **Goal:** Be able to write every Physics equation in the 0654 syllabus from memory with its symbols and units (there is no equation sheet), and fix the three weakest Physics topics from your error log.

## Before you start

- Your error log from all past-paper sessions, with the **three Physics topics** where you lost most marks across both Paper 4 attempts (and Papers 2 and 6 if you did them).
- The Round 1 Physics pages for those topics (phys-r1-01 to phys-r1-12).
- Blank paper, a timer, a scientific calculator. Optional: an unused 0654 Paper 2 or Paper 4 (current syllabus, 2025 onwards) to pull extra questions on your weak topics.

## Exam format reminder

- **Paper 2** (Multiple Choice, Extended): 45 min, 40 marks, 30% — 40 four-option questions across Biology, Chemistry and Physics.
- **Paper 4** (Theory, Extended): 2 h, 120 marks, 50% — structured questions across the three sciences.
- **Paper 6** (Alternative to Practical): 1 h 30 min, 60 marks, 20%.
- **No equations are given in any paper.** Every "recall and use" equation below can be tested directly. The papers only remind you that the weight of 1.0 kg is 9.8 N (g = 9.8 m/s²).

## How to do it

1. **0–15 min — equation test.** Cover the sheet below. On blank paper write every equation from memory with what each symbol means and its unit. Then check: tick, or correct in red.
2. **15–25 min — learn the gaps.** Write each missed equation out three times and make a flashcard (equation on one side, symbols and units on the other). Do one quick calculation with it.
3. **25–55 min — weak topics.** 10 minutes per topic: reread the Notes of its Round 1 page, then answer two practice questions from that page (or a past paper) without looking. Mark them at once.
4. **55–60 min — retest.** Write out the equations you missed in step 1 again from memory. Update the error log.

## Exam technique for this paper

- Write the equation **in symbols** before substituting — that line alone often earns the C1.
- Rearrange before substituting; use the triangle method only if you are sure.
- Convert to base units first: m, kg, s, A, m² for area, m³ for volume (kW h is the exception: kW × hours).
- Check the answer is sensible (a car at 300 m/s? a person of mass 7 kg?) and has a unit.
- Do not use equations that are not in 0654 (e.g. p = mv, c = ΔE ÷ mΔθ, pV = constant, n = 1 ÷ sin c, H₀ = v ÷ d): 0654 questions are written so you never need them.

## Checklist while marking

- For every equation you missed: is it a **recall** problem (did not remember it) or a **use** problem (remembered it but rearranged or converted wrongly)? Recall → flashcards every day until the mock. Use → three quick calculations with it.
- For each weak topic: after the practice questions, give yourself a confidence score 1–5 and record it. Revisit anything below 4 before 11 January.
