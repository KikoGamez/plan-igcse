# Weak topics and equations

> **Goal:** Learn every equation the syllabus requires (there is no formula sheet), with symbols and units, and fix the three weakest topics from your error log.

## Before you start

- Your error log from the Paper 2, Paper 4 and Paper 6 sessions; list the **three topics** where you lost most marks.
- The Round 1 topic pages for those topics (phys-r1-01 to phys-r1-12).
- Blank paper, a timer, a calculator. Optional: a past Paper 4 from a series you have not done (e.g. Oct/Nov 2024 or May/June 2025) to pull extra questions on your weak topics.

## Exam format reminder

- Paper 2: 45 min, 40 multiple-choice questions (30%). Paper 4: 1 h 15, 80 marks structured (50%). Paper 6: 1 h, 40 marks Alternative to Practical (20%).
- **No equations are given in any paper.** Every "recall and use" equation below can be tested directly.

## How to do it

1. **0–15 min — equation test.** Cover the sheet below. On blank paper write every equation from memory, with what each symbol means and its unit. Then check: tick, or correct in red.
2. **15–25 min — learn the gaps.** For each one you missed, write it out 3 times and make a flashcard (equation on one side, symbols and units on the other).
3. **25–55 min — weak topics.** 10 minutes per topic: re-read the Notes section of its Round 1 page, then answer two practice questions from that page (or from a past paper) without looking.
4. **55–60 min — retest.** Write out the equations you missed in step 1 again from memory. Update the error log.

## Exam technique for this paper

- Write the equation **in symbols** before substituting — that line alone often earns a mark.
- Rearrange before substituting when you can; use the triangle method only if you are sure.
- Convert to base units first: m, kg, s, A, K for gas/temperature questions, m² for area, m³ for volume.
- Check the answer is sensible (a car at 300 m/s? a person of mass 7 kg?) and has a unit.

## Checklist while marking

- For every equation you missed: is it a **recall** problem (didn't remember it) or a **use** problem (remembered but rearranged/converted wrongly)? Recall → flashcards daily. Use → do three quick calculations with it.
- For each weak topic: after the practice questions, give yourself a confidence score 1–5 and record it. Revisit anything below 4 before the mock.
