# Past paper · Paper 1 condensed

> **Goal:** do the core of a real Paper 1 Reading in 45 minutes under exam conditions, then mark it with the official mark scheme in 15 minutes.

## Before you start

- **Paper:** a recent 0502 Paper 1 (e.g. May/June or Oct/Nov 2024 or 2025) that you have **not** done before, with its **insert** (the reading texts) and **mark scheme**. Get them from your teacher or the school's Cambridge resources; if none is available, ask your teacher for a specimen paper for the current syllabus.
- Timer, two pens, a highlighter, your error log.
- No dictionary — the real exam does not allow one.
- Read the **front page** first: duration, total marks, number of questions, word guidance. Write these down; they are your reference for the mock.

## Exam format reminder

- Paper 1 Reading is typically a **2-hour** paper (confirm on the front page) based on several unseen texts in an insert.
- It tests: **comprehension** of explicit and implicit meaning (short questions), a **summary** in your own words, **analysis of the writer's language** and its effect, and an **extended response to reading** (e.g. letter, diary, speech, interview based on a text).
- Reading time is included — no separate reading time is given.
- Rough timing in the full exam: allocate time **in proportion to the marks** (about 1.3–1.5 minutes per mark), leaving 5 minutes to check.

## How to do it

This is a **condensed** session: you will not finish the whole paper. Choose the sections that give the most practice.

| Time | What to do |
|---|---|
| 0–3 min | Read the front page and all the questions. Note the marks for each part. |
| 3–18 min | **Comprehension questions** on the first text: read the text, then answer. |
| 18–33 min | **Summary** (plan in note form in your own words → write in continuous prose). |
| 33–45 min | **Writer's language question**: choose words/phrases, write meaning + effect. *(Alternatively, if this is your weakest area, swap this block for the extended response plan + first half.)* |
| 45–60 min | **Mark** with the mark scheme (see checklist below). |

Do the remaining question(s) in another session or as homework under timed conditions, so that you have attempted the whole paper before the mock.

## Exam technique for this paper

**Comprehension**
- One point per mark; for [2] give two clearly different ideas.
- When asked *con sus propias palabras*, paraphrase — copying is not credited.
- Stay within the lines/paragraphs named.

**Summary**
- Underline the **focus**; select only relevant ideas.
- No introduction, examples, quotations or personal opinions.
- Continuous prose, grouped ideas, linking words, within the word guidance.

**Writer's language**
- Short quotations; for each: **meaning** in context + **effect** on the reader (connotations, atmosphere).
- Naming a device without explaining it earns little.
- Cover all the paragraphs indicated; quality beats quantity.

**Extended response**
- Adopt the voice and form asked for (e.g. you are a character writing a diary).
- Use and **develop** details from the text — infer feelings and reasons, do not just retell.
- Cover **all** bullet points in the task, roughly equally.
- Register appropriate to the form and audience.

## Checklist while marking

- Mark **strictly**: if the mark scheme says a point must be in your own words and you copied it, no mark.
- For comprehension: tick each point that matches the mark scheme's list; note alternatives accepted.
- For the summary: count the content points you hit; then judge the writing (own words, concision, organisation) against the level descriptors.
- For the language question: for each choice, did you give meaning **and** effect? Compare your best explanation with the scheme's indicative content; place yourself in a level.
- Record in your **error log**: question type · what went wrong (e.g. *copied phrase*, *only one idea for [2]*, *effect too generic*, *missed tilde*) · the fix for next time.
- Count spelling/accent errors in your answers: this matters for the writing marks.

## Quick-fire revision

- *Explique con sus propias palabras* = paraphrase.
- Marks in brackets = number of points needed.
- Summary: focus → select → reword → group → write. No introduction or opinion.
- Language: quote short · meaning · effect · connotation · overall atmosphere.
- Extended response: voice, all bullets, develop with inference, correct form.
- Tildes in past tense (*llegó, miró*), interrogatives in indirect questions (*no sabía qué hacer*), opening ¿ ¡.
- Leave 5 minutes to proofread.
