# Past paper · Paper 2 condensed

> **Goal:** write one full Paper 2 task under timed conditions (directed writing **or** composition) and plan the other, then mark both against the level descriptors.

## Before you start

- **Paper:** a recent 0502 Paper 2 (e.g. May/June or Oct/Nov 2024 or 2025) that you have **not** done, with its reading material for the directed writing and the **mark scheme** (level descriptors). Ask your teacher if you do not have one; a specimen paper for the current syllabus also works.
- Timer, pens, a highlighter, your error log and your notes from spa-r1-04, spa-r1-05 and spa-r1-06.
- No dictionary.
- Read the **front page**: duration, total marks, marks for each section, and the word guidance for each task. Note them down.

## Exam format reminder

- Paper 2 Writing is typically a **2-hour** paper (confirm on the front page).
- **Directed writing:** read one or two short texts and write a letter, article or speech (or similar) that uses and **evaluates** their ideas. Marked for reading (selection, evaluation, development of ideas) and writing (register, structure, style, accuracy).
- **Composition:** choose **one** title — descriptive or narrative. Marked for content and structure, and for style and accuracy.
- In the full exam, split the time roughly in proportion to the marks for each section, keeping about 10 minutes for planning and 5 for checking in each.

## How to do it

Pick the task you are **weaker** at for the full timed answer.

| Time | Option A: directed writing in full | Option B: composition in full |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Read the texts; F-A-P-R (forma, audiencia, propósito, registro); highlight ideas in both texts | Read all titles; choose one you can see clearly |
| 5–10 min | Paragraph plan: intro · ideas from text A evaluated · text B evaluated/compared · counterargument · conclusion | Plan: 5 paragraphs (descriptive camera zoom, or planteamiento–nudo–clímax–desenlace) |
| 10–40 min | Write | Write |
| 40–45 min | Proofread: accents, commas, register | Proofread: tenses, accents, dialogue raya |
| 45–50 min | **Plan only** a composition title (5 bullet points) | **Plan only** the directed writing (F-A-P-R + 4 evaluated ideas) |
| 50–60 min | Mark with the level descriptors | Mark with the level descriptors |

## Exam technique for this paper

**Directed writing**
- Use ideas from **both** texts, in your own words.
- **Evaluate**: *este argumento resulta poco convincente porque…*, *el texto B ofrece datos más sólidos…*.
- Clear opinion from the introduction to the conclusion.
- Form conventions: letter (*Estimado/a…:* / *Atentamente,*), article (title, rhetorical questions), speech (direct address, *Muchas gracias por su atención*).

**Descriptive composition**
- No plot; build atmosphere with the five senses and precise vocabulary.
- Structure by space or time; finish with a strong closing image.

**Narrative composition**
- One main conflict, few characters, a striking opening, controlled tension, a meaningful ending (never "it was a dream").
- Indefinido for actions, imperfecto for background; raya for dialogue.

**Both**
- Vary sentence length; use imagery sparingly and originally.
- Accuracy can move you up or down a band: tildes, commas, ¿ ¡, *haber/a ver*, *porque/por qué*.

## Checklist while marking

- Read the **level descriptors** in the mark scheme for each assessment strand (e.g. content/structure and style/accuracy). Decide the level that **best fits**, then the mark within it.
- Directed writing: did you use both texts? Did you **evaluate** or just repeat? Is the register consistent?
- Composition: is a descriptive piece really descriptive (no story)? Does the narrative have a clear structure and ending?
- Underline every spelling, accent and punctuation error. More than a few per paragraph will hold you back a band.
- Error log: task · weakness (e.g. *no evaluation*, *weak ending*, *tense mixing*, *comma before verb*) · one concrete fix · one sentence rewritten correctly.

## Quick-fire revision

- F-A-P-R before writing any directed task.
- Evaluate, compare, conclude; both texts, own words.
- Carta formal: *Estimado/a señor/a…:* — dos puntos; *Atentamente,*.
- Descriptive = senses + precise nouns/verbs + structure by zoom/time.
- Narrative = in medias res, show don't tell, tension, real ending.
- Dialogue: *—Ya voy —dijo Ana—. Espérame.*
- No tilde: *solo, este, guion, fue, dio, ti*. Tilde: *más, aún* (= todavía), *qué/cómo* in indirect questions, *país, río*.
- Lowercase days, months, nationalities.
- Always leave time to proofread.
