How to study each hour

Method

Round 1 session (60 min)

By the end of the hour, the topic should be on a summary sheet and in flashcards. Questions come from topical past-paper questions.

Round 2 session (60 min)

Long papers (2 h) take two sessions in a row. Always mark with the official mark scheme, never by guesswork.

  1. Error log. Every wrong answer is written down with the correct answer and why it was wrong. Round 2 revision works from here.
  2. Active recall, not re-reading. Cover your notes and explain the topic out loud, or use flashcards (Anki or paper).
  3. Weekly review on Sundays. The last hour of each Sunday goes back over the whole week.
  4. Short breaks. On 4-hour days, 10 minutes between hours and a longer break halfway.
  5. If a day is missed, do not pile it up: catch up in the Sunday review or on a Round 2 day with a lighter load for that subject.
  6. Mark schemes and examiner reports. Learn which exact words earn marks in science (“diffusion is the net movement…”).
